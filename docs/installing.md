# Should I "install" the Keyguard Designer, or just use it in my browser?

*A plain-language guide for clinicians.*

When you open the Keyguard Designer, your browser may offer to "install" it (often
a small icon in the address bar, or a menu option like "Install app"). Installing
doesn't download a traditional program and doesn't require special permissions in
most cases — it simply gives the web app its own icon and window, like a regular
app. You can use the designer perfectly well **without** installing it. Here's the
trade-off.

## Advantages of installing it

- **It gets its own icon.** You can launch it from your desktop, taskbar, or Start
  menu/Dock instead of finding a bookmark in your browser.
- **It opens in its own clean window** — no browser tabs, address bar, or other
  sites in the way. Less clutter, fewer accidental clicks.
- **More reliable offline.** Once you've opened it at least once, the app keeps
  working even without an internet connection. Installing makes this behavior more
  dependable, which is handy on spotty clinic Wi-Fi.
- **Feels like a normal app**, which some people find simpler to return to day
  after day.

## Disadvantages of installing it

- **Your work computer may not allow it.** On computers managed by an IT
  department, "install" is sometimes blocked. If so, that's fine — just use it in
  the browser instead. Nothing is lost; it's the same app either way.
- **Updates take an extra step.** When a new version is released, a browser tab
  picks it up the next time you refresh the page. The installed version also
  updates automatically, but you may need to fully **close and reopen the app
  window** (occasionally twice) before you see the newest version. It's not
  harder, just slightly less obvious.
- **One more icon to manage.** If you stop using it, you'd remove the icon —
  though uninstalling is instant and removes nothing of your work.

## Does installing change anything about my designs or files?

No. Either way, the app works with keyguard files in a folder you choose on your
own computer. Your designs, presets, and files live on your computer, not in the
app, and are completely unaffected by whether you install it or not.
Uninstalling never deletes any of your work.

## Bottom line

Installing is purely about convenience — a tidy icon and a cleaner window. If it's
offered and you'd like quick access, go ahead. If your computer blocks it or you'd
rather not, just bookmark the page and use it in your browser; you lose nothing.
